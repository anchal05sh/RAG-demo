FAQ: Returns

Q: What is your return policy?
A: We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for unused items in their original condition and packaging.

Q: How do I start a return?
A: Contact support with your order number and reason for the return. We will provide return instructions.

Q: Are sale items returnable?
A: Most sale items are eligible for return unless the product listing specifically says they are final sale.

Q: Who pays for return shipping?
A: Customers generally pay return shipping for change-of-mind returns. If an item is defective or incorrect, contact support before shipping it back.

Q: When will I receive my refund?
A: Refunds are normally processed within 5–7 business days after the returned item is inspected.
