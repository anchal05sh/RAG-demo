FAQ: Gift Wrapping

Q: Do you offer gift wrapping?
A: Yes. Gift wrapping is available for eligible products during checkout.

Q: How much does gift wrapping cost?
A: Gift wrapping is offered for a small additional fee, which is shown during checkout.

Q: Can I add a gift message?
A: Yes. You can enter a short gift message when gift wrapping is selected.

Q: Can I gift wrap an order after placing it?
A: Contact support as soon as possible. Gift wrapping may be added only if the order has not yet been processed.

Q: Will the recipient see the price?
A: Gift orders do not normally include a printed price on the gift message or packing slip.
