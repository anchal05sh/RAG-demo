FAQ: Passwords

Q: I forgot my password. What should I do?
A: Select “Forgot password” on the sign-in page and follow the password-reset instructions sent to your registered email address.

Q: How do I change my password?
A: Sign in, open Account Settings, select Security, and choose Change Password.

Q: What makes a strong password?
A: Use a long, unique password that is not reused on other websites. A password manager can help create and store unique passwords.

Q: Can support tell me my password?
A: No. Support will never ask you to disclose your password or send it to us.

Q: What if I suspect my account was compromised?
A: Reset your password immediately, sign out of other sessions if available, and contact support through the official support channel.
